## 2026-09-01T19:11:56Z — [COMMUNICATION] docs(caveman-eval): output_tokens includes thinking and cannot be separated

§8 found hmd_ultra's tokens-per-visible-word rose 2.88 -> 3.54 after the
header-ban rule while the untouched control held flat, with four prompts
producing SHORTER visible text but MORE billed tokens. Hypothesis was
that added negative-constraint density inflates hidden thinking.

Answered documentarily from Anthropic's usage object: there is no
thinking/reasoning field. Only input_tokens, cache_creation_input_tokens,
cache_read_input_tokens, output_tokens — and thinking is billed the same
under every setting whether displayed or not. Thinking is INSIDE
output_tokens and the API offers no decomposition.

The planned paid probe was cancelled as pointless: sampling cannot split
two quantities the provider reports as one number.

Consequence for every figure in this doc: the harness measures BILLED
tokens. Cost claims stand. Conciseness claims do NOT — a rule can move
visible text down while moving thinking up. The tokens-per-visible-word
ratio is a proxy for the split, not a decomposition, and a rise in it is
a warning even when total tokens fall.

Hardens the design rule now supported by two independent findings (this
and the rejected ladder): prefer FEWER constraints covering more ground
over more negative rules. Future ultra work reports constraint COUNT with
byte count and treats a rising ratio as a regression signal.
