## 2026-08-23T04:33:01Z — [COMMUNICATION] docs(spec): confirm the Gatekeeper block by GUI, and it is worse than assessed

The consent-installer spec inferred from `spctl --assess` (rejected, rc=3, with and
without quarantine) that an ad-hoc-signed .app would fail a human double-click, and
honestly flagged that as UNVERIFIED since a headless session cannot click.

The disposable test.app escaped the scratchpad and the repo owner double-clicked it on
macOS 26.5.2. Confirmed, and two details make it worse than predicted:

  "test" Not Opened — Apple could not verify "test" is free of malware that may harm
  your Mac or compromise your privacy.   [ Move to Trash ]  [ Done ]

There is NO "Open Anyway" and no Control-click escape in the dialog. The historic
right-click→Open workaround that made ad-hoc distribution merely annoying is absent;
proceeding requires knowing to visit System Settings → Privacy & Security, which the
dialog never mentions. And the destructive option is the visually dominant one, so the
default path for a non-expert is to delete what they just installed.

macOS also relocated the bundle into AppTranslocation, its read-only quarantine sandbox
— evidence the attempt reached full Gatekeeper enforcement, not just a signature check.

This strengthens the standing recommendation rather than changing it. An ad-hoc-signed
.app is not awkward to distribute, it is undistributable to strangers. So $99/yr buys a
real thing on the Gatekeeper side — and still buys nothing on the TCC side, where the
actual 41-day outage lived. Stay CLI.

## 2026-08-23T04:37:50Z — [COMMUNICATION] Merge branch 'worktree-agent-a9e8c2f7ade79ea76' — reconnect the /hmd:maintain seek-then-fix loop

One label set severed the pipeline. seeker filed issues as 'bug,seeker'; the engine's
sync_queue_from_github requests only 'maintainer' (DEFAULT_MAINTAINER_LABEL). gh's
--label filter is a SUPERSET/AND match, not OR, so the two sets never intersected and a
seeker-filed issue was never once ingested. That is why both halves of /hmd:maintain
measured zero spawns across 432 agent calls — not disinterest, a broken connection.

Fixed on the seeker side rather than by widening the engine filter, and the reasoning
matters: 'bug' already caps severity at high (never critical), so auto-ingest does not
newly trigger the approval-park gate, and the fixer's hard PR-not-merge boundary keeps a
human reviewing before anything lands. The routing matrix is severity x confidence with
no origin gate, so auto-ingest is the intended design, not something needing a manual
promotion step.

The regression guard is the valuable part: a label-aware fake gh (the shared fake ignores
--label entirely, which is exactly how this went unnoticed) pins that the historical
bug,seeker set ingests 0.

Conflict resolution note: both this branch and an earlier merge corrected the same false
'normalized by issue_queue' claim in commands/maintain.md. Kept main's wording, and fixed
a contradiction the branch left behind — it still told readers a seeker-filed issue is
'not automatically picked up' and pointed at a manual workaround, which its own fix had
just made false.
